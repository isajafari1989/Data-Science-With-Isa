How to use · keyboard shortcuts · tips
  • Start, then F for full screen. Read the line at the red marker.
  • Space or a tap = pause / play · ↑ ↓ = slower / faster · + − = text size
  • Mouse wheel, drag, or ← → = move back / forward · Home = back to the start
  • V = voice follow on / off · R = record / stop (camera mode) · Esc = back to editing

Voice follow listens through your microphone and keeps the word you're saying on the reading line. Skipped or misheard words are fine, and it never jumps backwards because of noise. If you go back on purpose, drag or press ← and it follows you from there. It works best in Chrome or Edge and needs internet, because the browser sends the audio to its speech service. In other browsers, the text scrolls at your chosen speed instead.

Camera mode shows your camera full screen with the script in a band at the top, close to the lens. Press ● Rec to record. The file has only your camera and microphone, not the text. When you stop, you can download the take. Chrome on a laptop works when you open this file directly. On a phone, use the hosted link (https), because phones only allow the camera on secure web pages.

Tips: put the camera at eye level, about 1 to 1.5 m away, and keep the text band small and near the top so your eyes stay close to the lens.
